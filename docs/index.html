Multi-Region Scanner

Parallel scanning across all 9 AWS regions simultaneously. Detects misconfigurations across:

  • S3 buckets — public access, versioning, encryption
  • EC2 instances — security groups, public exposure
  • IAM users, roles, policies, and access keys
  • RDS databases — encryption and public access
  • VPC flow logs, NACLs, and route tables
  • KMS key rotation and compliance
  • CloudTrail logging and trail integrity
⚡

AI-Planned Remediation

One-click fixes for every finding. CloudShield generates a remediation plan using AI, shows you exactly what will change, and applies it only after your approval.

  • Preview every change before execution
  • Approval-gated — never changes without consent
  • Supports batch remediation of multiple findings
  • Tracks every action with full audit trail
🛡

Live Threat Monitor

Real-time WebSocket-powered monitoring that alerts you the moment a new misconfiguration or security drift is detected in your AWS environment.

  • Zero-delay real-time alerts via WebSocket
  • Severity-based prioritization (Critical/High/Medium/Low)
  • Email notifications for critical threats
  • Continuous drift detection between scans
↩️

Full Rollback Engine

Made a mistake? Every remediation CloudShield applies is fully reversible. Restore your exact previous AWS configuration with a single click.

  • Immutable log of every change made
  • One-click restore to pre-remediation state
  • Rollback history preserved indefinitely
  • Works across all supported AWS services
🗺️

Attack Surface Mapping

Visualize your entire exposed attack surface in one dashboard. See every public-facing resource, open port, and unencrypted asset at a glance.

  • Public IPs and internet-exposed EC2 instances
  • Publicly accessible S3 buckets
  • Unencrypted RDS instances
  • Overprivileged IAM roles and policies
🔑

IAM Deep Audit

Complete visibility into your IAM posture. Inspect every user, role, and policy for overpermissions, stale credentials, and MFA gaps.

  • Unused access keys and stale credentials
  • Users without MFA enabled
  • Overly permissive policies (Admin wildcards)
  • Cross-account role trust analysis
📊

Security Analytics

Track your security posture over time with detailed trend charts, severity breakdowns, and compliance framework coverage reports.

  • Historical posture score trending
  • Severity distribution charts
  • Per-service finding breakdown
  • Downloadable HTML compliance reports
📋

Scan History & Audit Trail

Every scan, finding, and remediation is permanently logged. Filter by service, severity, region, or date to investigate any past event.

  • Full scan result history
  • Per-finding lifecycle tracking
  • Remediation & rollback timeline
  • Export to CSV or HTML

CloudShield scans all 9 regions in parallel across 8 AWS services — results in <30 seconds.

Remediate & Monitor
